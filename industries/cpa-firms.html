What we automate

Five processes that eat your team’s hours without needing their judgment.

We start with work that is high-volume, rules-based and done the same way every week. That is where automation returns time fastest, and where your senior staff should not be spending theirs.

01

Client document collection

Per-client checklists, portal requests and reminders that keep going until the file is complete. Your team stops writing “just following up” emails.

02

Bank and card reconciliation

Transactions pulled from bank feeds, categorized against the client’s chart of accounts and matched to statements. Only the items that don’t match reach a person.

03

Engagement letters and deadlines

Engagement letters issued and tracked to signature. Filing and extension deadlines calendared per client and entity, with reminders before anything is due.

04

Invoice and payables processing

Invoices read from email or upload, coded, matched to purchase orders where they exist, and queued for approval in the client’s books.

05

Client status updates

“Where is my return?” answered with the real status from your workflow tool, so partners stop fielding status calls in peak season.

FAQs

Questions accounting firms ask us first.

Will this work with our accounting and practice-management software?
We build on top of the tools you already use rather than replacing them. Agents connect through your systems’ APIs where they exist, and through email, client portals and shared drives where they don’t. We confirm exactly what connects, and how, on the discovery call.
Does the AI make accounting or tax judgments?
No. Agents handle collection, matching, coding suggestions, reminders and status updates. Anything that needs professional judgment, such as unusual classifications, tax positions or advice to a client, goes to a qualified person. You set the approval thresholds, and nothing is posted, filed or sent to a client above them without a person approving it.
How is our data protected?
Upcore is ISO 27001 certified for information security and ISO 9001 certified for quality management, and delivers to CMMI Level 3 defined processes. Agents run with scoped, revocable permissions, every agent action is logged, and changes are reversible. Where your policies require it, we deploy into your own cloud tenancy, and we configure model providers so your data is not used to train their models.
Can we start before our busy season?
That is the best time to start. We set up and test the document-collection agent on a small group of clients first, tune it to your checklists and wording, then extend it to everyone before the peak arrives.
How long until the first agent is live?
Our standard is a first agent live within 30 days of design sign-off. We map the workflow with your team, agree the approval rules, then build and test against real scenarios before anything is switched on. Further agents reuse the same connections, so each one is faster than the last.
What does it cost?
It depends on the workflows, volumes and systems involved, so we don’t publish a price list. After the discovery call we send a written proposal with a fixed scope and price before any build starts.
