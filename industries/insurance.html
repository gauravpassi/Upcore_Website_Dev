FAQs

Questions insurers and brokers ask us first.

Does the AI decide claims or underwriting outcomes?
No. Agents capture, extract, chase and route. Coverage decisions, claim settlements and underwriting judgments stay with your licensed staff. You set the thresholds for what an agent may do on its own, and everything it does is logged.
Does it work for brokers as well as carriers and MGAs?
Yes. The processes overlap, but the configuration differs: brokers lean on renewals, certificates and submission preparation, while carriers and MGAs lean on first notice of loss, claims follow-up and submission triage. We start from whichever workflow costs you the most time.
Which systems do you integrate with?
We work with your policy administration, claims, CRM and email systems rather than replacing them, connecting through APIs where they exist and at document level where they don’t. We confirm exactly what connects on the discovery call.
Can it take claims outside business hours?
Yes. Intake runs around the clock on the channels you choose, creates a structured claim record, and follows your rules for what can wait until morning and what goes to an on-call adjuster immediately.
How is policyholder data protected?
Upcore is ISO 27001 certified for information security and ISO 9001 certified for quality management, and delivers to CMMI Level 3 defined processes. Agents run with scoped, revocable permissions, every agent action is logged, and changes are reversible. Where your policies require it, we deploy into your own cloud tenancy, and we configure model providers so your data is not used to train their models.
What does it cost?
It depends on the workflows, volumes and systems involved, so we don’t publish a price list. After the discovery call we send a written proposal with a fixed scope and price before any build starts.
