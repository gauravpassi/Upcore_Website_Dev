What we automate

Five processes that keep attorneys away from billable work.

We automate the administrative work around legal judgment: the intake, reading, docketing and record-keeping that has to be done right but doesn’t need a lawyer to do it.

01

Client intake and conflict checks

Inquiries captured from every channel, details extracted, and a conflict search run against your records before an attorney spends time on the call.

02

First-pass document and contract review

Contracts and productions read against your playbook. Non-standard clauses, missing terms and risk items flagged with the exact page reference, ready for attorney review.

03

Deadline and docket tracking

Dates extracted from filings, orders and correspondence, calendared against your rules, with escalating reminders to the responsible attorney and paralegal.

04

Time capture and billing narratives

Work recorded from emails, documents and calendar entries, turned into draft time entries with clean narratives for attorneys to approve before the bill goes out.

05

Client updates and document collection

Clients receive status updates at each matter milestone and are chased for the documents you need, without a paralegal writing the same email again.

FAQs

Questions law firms ask us first.

Is client confidentiality protected?
Upcore is ISO 27001 certified for information security and ISO 9001 certified for quality management, and delivers to CMMI Level 3 defined processes. Agents run with scoped, revocable permissions, every agent action is logged, and changes are reversible. Where your policies require it, we deploy into your own cloud tenancy, and we configure model providers so your data is not used to train their models.
Does the AI give legal advice?
No. Agents capture, extract, draft and remind. They prepare work for attorneys, who review and approve before anything reaches a client, a counterparty or a court. Supervision and sign-off stay exactly where your professional obligations require them.
How do you prevent AI from making things up?
Every issue an agent flags points to the exact document, page and clause it came from, so an attorney can verify it in seconds. Agents are configured to work from your documents and your playbook rather than general knowledge, outputs are logged, and anything an agent can’t ground in a source is surfaced as a question, not an answer.
Will it work with our practice-management and document systems?
We build on top of what you already run. Agents connect through your systems’ APIs where they exist, and through email and shared folders where they don’t. We confirm exactly what connects on the discovery call.
How long until the first agent is live?
Our standard is a first agent live within 30 days of design sign-off. We map the workflow with your team, agree the approval rules, then build and test against real scenarios before anything is switched on. Further agents reuse the same connections, so each one is faster than the last.
What does it cost?
It depends on the workflows, volumes and systems involved, so we don’t publish a price list. After the discovery call we send a written proposal with a fixed scope and price before any build starts.
