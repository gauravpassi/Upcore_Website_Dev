Agent Specialities

Your AI Workforce for Manufacturing

Agent 01

Quality Control

Processes inspection data in real-time, surfaces defect patterns by batch and shift, and generates CAPA suggestions with full context for your team's review and approval. catching problems at source before they ship.

Agent 02

Procurement Intelligence

Tracks commodity prices daily, monitors supplier delivery and quality performance, flags cost movements and single-source dependencies, and alerts your procurement team before price commitments are made.

Agent 03

Supply Chain Monitor

Monitors inbound and outbound logistics, surfaces bottlenecks and delays in real-time, tracks inventory levels against demand forecasts, and escalates supply chain risks before they impact production.

Agent 04

Predictive Maintenance

Analyzes equipment sensor data and maintenance logs, predicts failure patterns, generates maintenance schedules optimized for production, and surfaces early warnings, reducing unplanned downtime and emergency repairs.

Agent 05

Hydrogen Feasibility

Analyzes site data, models CAPEX, OPEX and ROI, reviews regulatory constraints and incentives, and produces board-ready feasibility reports in days. accelerating decision-making on energy and infrastructure projects.
