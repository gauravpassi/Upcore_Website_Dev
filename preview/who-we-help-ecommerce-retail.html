Upcore framework · Consumer behaviour

Less effort for customers means fewer tickets for you.

Customers judge you on how easy it is to get a simple answer. We design every retail agent to remove steps, not just to reply faster.

The effort principle

Customers don’t stay loyal because you delighted them once. They leave when getting a simple answer takes too much effort.

So we design agents to remove steps: the answer comes to the customer, on the channel they already use, with live data behind it.

  • Fewer steps for the customer, fewer tickets for your team
  • Answers from live store, ERP and carrier data
  • Exceptions handed to a person with the full context

Grounded in customer-effort research: Dixon, Freeman & Toman, “Stop Trying to Delight Your Customers”, Harvard Business Review, 2010.

FAQs

Questions retail teams ask us first.

Anything else? The discovery call is the fastest way to find out whether automation will pay off for you.

Do we have to replace our helpdesk?

No. Agents work alongside Gorgias, Zendesk, Freshdesk or your own inbox, and connect to Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, your ERP and your carriers through their APIs. We confirm exactly what connects on the discovery call.

Can it handle bulky, made-to-order or white-glove delivery?

Yes, and that is where off-the-shelf tracking tools fall short. We connect to your production schedule, 3PL and delivery partners, so customers get real answers about build and delivery dates, not just a parcel tracking link.

Will customers know they are talking to an agent?

You decide. Most clients disclose it and keep the tone in their brand voice. Anything sensitive, such as refunds, complaints or high-value orders, goes to a person with the full context attached.

Can we go live before peak season?

That is the best time to start. We run the agent on a share of conversations first, tune it to your policies and tone, then scale it up before the peak arrives.

How is our data protected?

Upcore is ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 certified and delivers to CMMI Level 3 processes. Agents run with scoped, revocable permissions, every action is logged and reversible, and where your policies require it we deploy into your own cloud. We configure model providers so your data is not used to train their models.

What does it cost?

It depends on the workflows, volumes and systems involved, so we don’t publish a price list. After the discovery call you get a written proposal with a fixed scope and price before any build starts.
