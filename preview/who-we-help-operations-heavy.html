Upcore framework · Operations science

The weeks are in the waiting, not the work.

Most operational lead time is spent waiting on replies, documents and approvals. Agents remove the waiting; your people keep the decisions.

Lead-time Compression

Most process time is not work, it is waiting: for a reply, a document, a signature, a bank. Agents don’t make your people work faster; they remove the waiting between the steps.

That is where weeks come back. For one residential developer, time from booking to first installment fell from 6–10 weeks to about 3.

  • Work stays with people; chasing goes to agents
  • Measured end to end, not task by task
  • Less work in progress means faster cash and fewer status calls

Grounded in Little’s Law: J. D. C. Little, “A Proof for the Queuing Formula: L = λW”, Operations Research, 1961.

FAQs

Questions COOs ask us first.

Anything else? The discovery call is the fastest way to find out whether automation will pay off for you.

Which systems do you work with?

We build on what you already run: CRMs such as Salesforce, HubSpot and Zoho; ERPs; email; WhatsApp; shared drives and industry systems. Agents connect through APIs where they exist and through email and documents where they don’t.

Does the AI make decisions on its own?

Only within the limits you set. Agents handle the repetitive work; anything above your thresholds, such as write-offs, exceptions or customer disputes, goes to a named person. Every action is logged and reversible.

We are not a tech company. Who runs this after go-live?

We do, with your team. A monthly retainer covers monitoring, tuning and new workflows, and your team is trained to understand and own what has been built.

Which industries is this for?

Any business where growth means more follow-ups and documents. Our live work includes real estate developers, multi-location healthcare groups, staffing and compliance services.

How fast can we see results?

Our standard is a first agent live within 30 days of design sign-off. Further agents reuse the same connections, so each one is faster than the last.

What does it cost?

It depends on the workflows, volumes and systems involved, so we don’t publish a price list. After the discovery call you get a written proposal with a fixed scope and price before any build starts.
