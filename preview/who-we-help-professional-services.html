Upcore framework · Behavioural science

Autonomy is earned, one level at a time.

Professional firms cannot afford an agent that guesses. Every agent starts by drafting for your people to approve, and earns more autonomy only on measured evidence.

Autonomy Ladder

Trust in automation should be earned, not assumed. Too little and people redo the agent’s work; too much and errors slip through.

Every agent starts at Level 2, drafting work for a person to approve. It moves up only when its measured accuracy on your real cases justifies it, and you approve every promotion.

  • Five levels, from manual to autonomous with sampled audit
  • Promotion on measured accuracy, never by default
  • Any agent can be stepped back down at any time

Derived from: Parasuraman, Sheridan & Wickens, “A Model for Types and Levels of Human Interaction with Automation”, IEEE Trans. SMC-A, 2000; Lee & See, “Trust in Automation: Designing for Appropriate Reliance”, Human Factors, 2004.

FAQs

Questions firms ask us first.

Anything else? The discovery call is the fastest way to find out whether automation will pay off for you.

Does the AI give professional advice?

No. Agents capture, chase, draft, check and remind. Accounting judgments, legal advice and financial recommendations stay with your qualified people, who approve anything that reaches a client, a court or a regulator.

How do you stop AI from making things up?

Agents work from your documents and records, not general knowledge. Every item they flag points to its source, outputs are logged, and anything they can’t ground in a source is raised as a question, not an answer.

Is client confidentiality protected?

Upcore is ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 certified and delivers to CMMI Level 3 processes. Agents run with scoped, revocable permissions, every action is logged, and where your policies require it we deploy into your own cloud. We configure model providers so your data is not used to train their models.

Will it work with our practice-management software?

We build on top of what you already run, connecting through APIs where they exist and through email, portals and shared folders where they don’t. We confirm exactly what connects on the discovery call.

Can it help with FCA Consumer Duty?

Yes. For advice firms we can assemble the client-outcome data your board report needs from your back-office system, and prepare review packs so ongoing reviews are delivered on time.

What does it cost?

It depends on the workflows, volumes and systems involved, so we don’t publish a price list. After the discovery call you get a written proposal with a fixed scope and price before any build starts.
