Global PCCS

Product compliance services for automotive suppliers · Bengaluru, India

≈$210Ka year of licensed tooling replaced
Before₹2 crore a year on licensed checking tools
AfterChecks run by the agent, no license spend
  1. Challenge

    Suppliers need an approved IMDS material data sheet before parts are accepted, so every sheet must be checked for wrong classifications, missing codes and restricted substances. The checking tools cost about ₹2 crore a year in licenses.

  2. What we built

    An automated quality and compliance-check agent that runs those checks inside the team’s own workflow.

  3. Result

    The checks now run through the agent, and the ₹2 crore (≈$210K) annual license spend is gone.

Oral surgery and dental implant network · 9 states, USA

10workflows automated, plus voice AI for doctors
Workflows for scheduling, intake and admin
Doctors dictate; notes and prescriptions are generated
