AI in production, not in a slide deck.

Ten engagements in engineering and automation, for clients in the US, UK, South Africa, Australia, Mauritius and India. Each in brief: the challenge, what we built and the result. Client names are withheld; ask us for a reference call.

Case studies

Automation
60%+fewer delivery-support tickets

Food & fashion retailer · ~960 stores · South Africa

Challenge
Online is one of the retailer’s fastest-growing channels. At that scale, every order can turn into a “where is my order?” support question.
What we built
Alongside leading four of the retailer’s mobile and ecommerce technology teams (2021–2023), we built a WhatsApp order-status and post-purchase agent that answers delivery questions automatically.
Result
Delivery-support tickets fell by more than 60%, with 10,000+ customer queries automated every month.

Automation
≈$210Ka year of licensed tooling replaced

Automotive product-compliance firm · India

Challenge
Suppliers need an approved IMDS material data sheet before parts are accepted, so every sheet must be checked for wrong classifications, missing codes and restricted substances. The checking tools cost about ₹2 crore a year in licenses.
What we built
An automated quality and compliance-check agent that runs those checks inside the team’s own workflow.
Result
The checks now run through the agent, and the ₹2 crore (≈$210K) annual license spend is gone.

Business Process AutomationOperations-heavy businesses

AutomationEngineering
10workflows automated, plus voice AI for doctors

Dental implant network · 9 states · United States

Challenge
Running scheduling, intake and admin the same way across clinics in nine states took heavy manual effort, and note-writing weighed on clinicians.
What we built
Ten workflows for scheduling, intake and admin with patient-sentiment tracking, and a voice tool: doctors speak instead of writing notes, speakers are separated automatically and prescriptions are generated.
Result
Staff are freed from repetitive admin and patient sentiment is tracked. The client hired us again to add test and DevOps agents to its release pipeline.
